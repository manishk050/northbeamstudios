Before your visit

Good to know.

A few details to help your appointment feel easy from the moment you arrive.

Hair color and weekend appointments are best requested two to three weeks ahead. Shorter services may have earlier availability.

Please give at least 24 hours’ notice if plans change. Late cancellations may be subject to the fee shared when your appointment is confirmed.

Nearby valet and metered street parking are available. Detailed arrival instructions are included with every appointment confirmation.

We select professional formulas around your hair, skin, and sensitivity needs. Your specialist will explain each product used and recommend only what supports your routine.

Your appointment begins with a focused consultation covering your goals, routine, sensitivities, and desired level of maintenance.

Walk-ins are welcome when the schedule permits, though appointments are recommended for specialist and longer services.

Digital and physical gift cards are available in custom amounts and may be used toward any eligible service.
