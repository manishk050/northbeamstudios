Frequently asked

Good questions are welcome.

What happens at a new patient visit?

The visit typically includes a conversation about your concerns, an oral exam, and a discussion of next steps. Imaging may be recommended when clinically appropriate.

Do you take dental insurance?

Benefit details can be checked before care. Coverage varies by plan, and treatment recommendations are based on clinical needs rather than benefit limits.

Are clear aligners right for everyone?

No. Suitability depends on tooth position, bite, oral health, and treatment goals. An assessment is needed before aligners can be recommended.

How much will my treatment cost?

After assessment, you would receive an itemized estimate for the agreed plan. The sample ranges above are illustrative only and not a substitute for a personal quote.

What if I feel anxious about dental visits?

Let the studio know when booking. The appointment can begin with a conversation about your concerns, communication preferences, and ways to make the visit feel more manageable.
